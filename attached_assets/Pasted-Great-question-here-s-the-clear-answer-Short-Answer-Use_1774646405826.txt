## Short Answer

**Use a subdomain of your client's own domain.** That's the cleanest and most professional setup.

---

## How It Looks in Practice

If your client's website is `rajenterprises.com`, their CDN would be:

```
Website:  rajenterprises.com
CDN:      cdn.rajenterprises.com
```

So their product images load from:
```
https://cdn.rajenterprises.com/products/shirt.jpg
```

---

## Why Not Use a Random Domain?

You *can* technically use any domain — but here's why you shouldn't:

| Option | Example | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Random domain | `cdn.myagency.com/client1` | Looks unprofessional, client sees your agency name |
| Replit's r2.dev URL | `pub-xxx.r2.dev/image.jpg` | Ugly, unbranded, slow |
| ✅ Client's subdomain | `cdn.client1.com` | Clean, fast, professional |

---

## One Important Thing to Know

For `cdn.rajenterprises.com` to work, **that domain must be on Cloudflare** — meaning you need to change its nameservers to Cloudflare's.

This means **one extra step per client:**

1. Client gives you access to their domain registrar (GoDaddy/Namecheap etc.)
2. You point the domain's nameservers to Cloudflare
3. Cloudflare takes over DNS
4. You create the `cdn.` subdomain pointing to R2

**This is a one-time 5 minute setup per client.**

## For Your Agency — The Smart Way to Handle This

Since you manage multiple clients, you have two clean options:

### Option A — Each client's own domain (Most Professional)
```
rajenterprises.com     → cdn.rajenterprises.com
sharma_sarees.com      → cdn.sharma_sarees.com
allahabad_sweets.com   → cdn.allahabad_sweets.com
```
Best if clients want full ownership of their brand.

### Option B — Your agency's CDN domain (Easiest to Manage)
```
Your agency owns:  agencymedia.com

Client 1 images → client1.agencymedia.com
Client 2 images → client2.agencymedia.com
Client 3 images → client3.agencymedia.com
```
Best if clients don't care about the image URL and you want one domain to manage everything.

## My Recommendation for You

Since you're just starting with 3 clients in a local market like Allahabad — **go with Option B first.** Buy one domain like `youragencycdn.com` (~₹800/year), set it up once on Cloudflare, and create subdomains for each client. Much faster to manage.

As you grow and clients start asking for full branding, switch them to Option A.
